# `factory` — interaction spec

Branch contract for the `factory` module of the `pairing` crate: the pairing family's construction point, admitting the concrete the composition names against the chain's declared precompile encodings and the suite's target-group encoding identifier and handing it to a consumer generic over `IPairingArithmetic` and `IPairingReference`. Each branch states condition, decision, dependency call, and the exact return outcome.

## `create_pairing<C: IPairingConsumer>(deps: &CreatePairingDeps<C>, params: CreatePairingParams, payload: CreatePairingPayload) -> CreatePairingReturn<C::Output>`

The decision is a `match` on `params.concrete`, one arm per `PairingConcrete` variant — `Bn254Arkworks`, `Bn254Halo2curves`, `Bls12381Arkworks`, `Bls12381Halo2curves` — exhaustive, so a variant with no arm fails to compile. Each arm constructs the concrete whose `CONCRETE` is the arm's variant and runs the same three-branch contract against it.

| Branch | Condition | Decision | Dependency call | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| unsupported encoding | the arm's concrete's `DECLARATION.precompile_encoding` is not in `params.supported_encodings` | `contains`, read before any construction | none | `Err(CreatePairingErrorReturn::UnsupportedPrecompileEncoding)`; nothing is constructed and the consumer is not called |
| unsupported target-group encoding | the arm's concrete's `DECLARATION.target_group_encoding` is not equal to `params.target_group_encoding` | equality, read after the encoding admission and before any construction | none | `Err(CreatePairingErrorReturn::UnsupportedTargetGroupEncoding)`; nothing is constructed and the consumer is not called |
| admitted | both admissions pass | both checks | the concrete's `try_new` with its fieldless constructor params, exactly once, its success destructured irrefutably because its error arm is uninhabited; then `deps.consumer.consume_pairing(ConsumePairingParams, ConsumePairingPayload { adapter })`, exactly once, the consumer reading `P::DECLARATION` and `P::CONCRETE` | `Ok(CreatePairingSuccessReturn { output })` holding the consumer's output |

Each concrete's own variant of `CreatePairingErrorReturn` — `Bn254Arkworks(Infallible)`, `Bn254Halo2curves(Infallible)`, `Bls12381Arkworks(Infallible)`, `Bls12381Halo2curves(Infallible)` — carries that concrete's constructor error in the return union unchanged; because every constructor error is `Infallible`, uninhabited, no branch produces one.

## Ordering and edges

- `params.concrete` selects the concrete; `params.supported_encodings` and `params.target_group_encoding` admit or refuse it. `payload` carries nothing and is not read.
- The encoding admission precedes the target-group admission and both precede construction in every arm: a refused concrete is never constructed, and `deps.consumer` is never touched.
- The consumer is generic over `P: IPairingArithmetic + IPairingReference`; it is instantiated inside the arm for the concrete the arm constructs and never names the concrete itself.
- Under `#[cfg(any(test, feature = "mocks"))]`, the mock arm: `params.concrete` of `PairingConcrete::Mock(mode)` takes the same decision, admission, and outcome as every arm, constructing the concrete by `MockIPairingAdapter::try_new(MockIPairingAdapterConstructorParams { failure_mode: mode })`, exactly once, with `MockIPairingAdapter::DECLARATION` as the declaration the admissions read.

## Integration: own entries

Each entry is named and proven by the private integration block of the same name in `integration_test.rs`, run per case over `PAIRING_CONCRETES` and `PairingConcrete::Mock(MockIPairingAdapterFailureMode::Succeeds)` — a failure-mode entry over the mock selection under its mode alone — and, where the public surface lists it, by the public integration block of the same name over `PAIRING_CONCRETES`. Every entry's params come from `build_create_pairing_params` naming only `concrete`, so the builder's defaults admit, and a consumer is written for the entry's work against the narrowest family trait that work needs.

- `the_consumer_receives_the_concrete_the_params_name`: condition `params.concrete` is a variant of `PAIRING_CONCRETES` and the admissions pass, the private block adding the case `PairingConcrete::Mock(MockIPairingAdapterFailureMode::Succeeds)`, whose `P::CONCRETE` is `Mock(Succeeds)` and so equals `params.concrete` as it does for every case; outcome `Ok` whose output is the consumer's reading of `P::CONCRETE`, equal to `params.concrete`; variation every variant in turn, so an arm that constructs a sibling concrete, including the other library of its curve, fails; edge a consumer written against `IPairingAdapter` alone is a valid implementation.
- `a_sampled_scalar_encodes_through_secret`: condition the consumer builds a `Secret<Vec<u8>>` of `P::Scalar::UNIFORM_BYTES_LENGTH` bytes, every byte zero but the last, which is `7`, samples it with `sample_from_uniform_bytes`, and encodes the sampled scalar with `encode_scalar`; outcome `Ok` whose output is the encoded scalar read through `Secret::expose`, 31 zero bytes then `7`, the 32 big-endian bytes of the integer seven a scalar below the group order encodes to; variation the nonzero last byte, which separates a sampled input from `build_secret`'s zero default and from a dropped input; edge the uniform input, the sampled scalar, and the encoding each cross the family inside `Secret` and are read only through `Secret::expose`.
- `a_sampling_refusal_reaches_the_caller_unchanged`: condition the consumer of `a_sampled_scalar_encodes_through_secret` given a uniform `Secret<Vec<u8>>` one byte shorter than `P::Scalar::UNIFORM_BYTES_LENGTH`; outcome `Ok` whose output is `Err(SampleUniformScalarErrorReturn::WrongLength { expected: 64, actual: 63 })`, the consumer propagating the sampling error unchanged; variation the shortened input against the full-length input of `a_sampled_scalar_encodes_through_secret`; edge the error crosses the consumer and the factory whole.
- `libraries_sharing_an_identifier_encode_the_pairing_product_to_the_same_bytes`: condition for every concrete, the consumer, written against `IPairingArithmetic`, samples a scalar from the uniform input of value `7`, multiplies the first-group generator by it with `mul_g1`, takes `pairing_product` over the terms of that point with the second-group generator and of the first-group generator with the second-group generator, and encodes the product with `encode_gt`; outcome the output pairs `P::DECLARATION.target_group_encoding` with the encoded product read through `Secret::expose`, and any two concretes whose identifiers are equal return equal bytes; variation a nonzero scalar and two terms, so the product is neither the target group's identity nor a single generator pairing; edge each library's correction of its reduced pairing to the identifier's exact value reaches the same serialized bytes.
- `libraries_sharing_an_identifier_return_the_same_scalar_field_order`: condition for every concrete, the consumer, written against `IPairingReference`, reads `scalar_field_order`; outcome the output pairs the identifier with the order's bytes, and any two concretes whose identifiers are equal return equal bytes; variation every concrete of the declared set, which holds each library of each identifier; edge the order is read through the family's surface alone.
- `libraries_sharing_an_identifier_return_the_same_first_group_reference_encoding`: condition for every concrete, the consumer, written against `IPairingReference`, reads `g1_outside_subgroup_encoding`, propagating its error unchanged; outcome the output pairs the identifier with the optional bytes, the bytes absent for `TargetGroupEncodingIdentifier::Bn254V1`, and present and equal across any two concretes whose identifiers are equal for `TargetGroupEncodingIdentifier::Bls12381V1`; variation both identifiers, so absence and presence are both exercised; edge the first group of BN254 has no point outside the subgroup, and the family returns no encoding for it.
- `libraries_sharing_an_identifier_return_the_same_second_group_reference_encoding`: condition for every concrete, the consumer, written against `IPairingReference`, reads `g2_outside_subgroup_encoding`, propagating its error unchanged; outcome the output pairs the identifier with the bytes, and any two concretes whose identifiers are equal return equal bytes; variation every concrete of the declared set; edge the encoding is the ascending search's least point outside the subgroup, so two libraries agree only if they search in the same order and encode alike.
- `each_second_group_decoder_refuses_the_second_group_reference_encoding`: condition for every concrete, the consumer, written against `IPairingReference`, reads `g2_outside_subgroup_encoding` and passes its bytes to the same concrete's `decode_g2`; outcome the output nests the reference's result around the decoder's result, each error carried unchanged, and equals `Ok(Err(DecodeG2ErrorReturn::NotInSubgroup))`; variation every concrete of the declared set; edge the decoder refuses a point on the curve and outside the subgroup, which its length, canonicality, and curve checks all pass.
- `each_first_group_decoder_refuses_the_first_group_reference_encoding_where_one_exists`: condition for every concrete, the consumer, written against `IPairingReference`, reads `g1_outside_subgroup_encoding` and, when it holds bytes, passes them to the same concrete's `decode_g1`; outcome the output nests the optional decoder result inside the reference's result, each error carried unchanged, and whenever the decoder ran its result is `Err(DecodeG1ErrorReturn::NotInSubgroup)`; variation both identifiers, so a concrete with no first-group encoding and a concrete with one are both run; edge the decoder refuses a point on the curve and outside the subgroup, which its length, canonicality, and curve checks all pass.
- `the_sample_scalar_wrong_length_mode_returns_the_sampling_refusal_whole`: condition the consumer samples `P::Scalar::UNIFORM_BYTES_LENGTH` uniform bytes on the concrete `PairingConcrete::Mock(MockIPairingAdapterFailureMode::SampleScalarWrongLength)` selects; outcome `Ok` whose output is `Err(SampleUniformScalarErrorReturn::WrongLength { expected: 64, actual: 64 })`, the family's flavor the mode manufactures because no input reaches the arm; variation the correctly-sized input under the mode, a refusal no real concrete's correctly-sized input produces; edge the failure mode the selection carries reaches `sample_from_uniform_bytes` through `MOCK_SAMPLING_MODE`, and the refusal arrives at the caller whole.

- `the_g1_outside_subgroup_search_exhausted_mode_returns_the_search_refusal_whole`: condition the consumer calls `g1_outside_subgroup_encoding` under `PairingConcrete::Mock(MockIPairingAdapterFailureMode::G1OutsideSubgroupSearchExhausted)`; outcome `Ok` whose output is `Err(G1OutsideSubgroupEncodingErrorReturn::SearchExhausted)`; variation the mode, the arm no input reaches on the mock concrete, whose first group is the whole curve; edge the refusal arrives at the caller whole in the family's flavor.
- `the_g2_outside_subgroup_search_exhausted_mode_returns_the_search_refusal_whole`: condition the consumer calls `g2_outside_subgroup_encoding` under `PairingConcrete::Mock(MockIPairingAdapterFailureMode::G2OutsideSubgroupSearchExhausted)`; outcome `Ok` whose output is `Err(G2OutsideSubgroupEncodingErrorReturn::SearchExhausted)`; variation and edge as the entry above.

## Integration: private surface

The chain of real functions is `create_pairing`, the arm's `try_new`, and the consumer's calls on the concrete through the family's traits, with `Secret::expose`, the `Secret` drop, and `AsRef<[u8]>` running for real. The cases are each variant of `PAIRING_CONCRETES` and `PairingConcrete::Mock(mode)`, `mode` `Succeeds` except where a failure-mode entry selects its own; no outer-edge collaborator is mocked, `domain` running as itself. The observable result is the `Result` `create_pairing` returns, `P::CONCRETE` read through the trait, each method's success and error values, and the encoded bytes; under the `libraries_sharing_*` entries the mock concrete contributes a `Mock`-identifier output that pairs with no other, and under `each_first_group_decoder_refuses_the_first_group_reference_encoding_where_one_exists` a `None`, so that entry's `Some(Err)` witness comes from the declared set. The entries proven are every own entry above.

## Integration: public surface

An outside caller invokes `create_pairing` from the crate's public surface with an `IPairingConsumer` it writes, the `CreatePairingParams` configuration supplies, and `CreatePairingPayload`, for each concrete in `PAIRING_CONCRETES`, and observes the consumer's output in `CreatePairingSuccessReturn`. The entries proven are the own entries above that run over the declared set, the failure-mode entries excluded, no input reaching their arms on a real concrete. The route of each is `create_pairing`, the arm's concrete through its `try_new`, and the consumer's calls on that concrete through the family's traits. The outer-edge collaborators on that route are none, and the fixtures are `build_secret`, `build_create_pairing_params`, and `build_create_pairing_deps`.
