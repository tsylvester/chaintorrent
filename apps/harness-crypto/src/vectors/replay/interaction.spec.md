# `replay` — interaction spec

Branch contract for the `replay` module of the `vectors` module of `apps/harness-crypto`: `replay` produces the replay vector records, generic over the credential KEM, the key agreement, and the delivery proof with its chain forms — per record a mint delivery from `harness-crypto/delivery/mint` over the typed mint context and the admitted identity, a transfer delivery from `harness-crypto/delivery/transfer` from the mint delivery's recipient to a buyer under the typed transfer context, and a transfer delivery from that buyer's holder to a next buyer under the typed next context, the next settlement of the same entitlement, each settlement's reference verdict, the reference verdict of the first transfer's proof against the next statement, and the deliveries' uniforms. Each branch states condition, decision, dependency call, and the exact return outcome. `F` below is `D::Forms`.

## `replay`

`replay<'a, P: IPairingAdapter, K: ICredentialKemAdapter<Pairing = P>, A: IKeyAgreementAdapter<Pairing = P>, D: IDeliveryProofAdapter<Pairing = P>>(deps: &ReplayDeps<'a, P, K, A, D>, params: ReplayParams, payload: ReplayPayload<'_, D::Forms>) -> ReplayReturn<P, D>`, the trusted form: every input is a typed value from the caller — the borrowed KEM, key agreement, delivery proof, and randomness source in `deps`, the count in `params`, the typed mint, transfer, and next contexts and the admitted identity in `payload`.

| Branch | Condition | Decision | Dependency call | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| not the next settlement | a field of `payload.next` the comparison reads differs from the `payload.transfer` value it is compared with | the first unequal pair in `NextSettlementField` order | none — decided before any record | `Err(ReplayErrorReturn::NotTheNextSettlement { field })`, `field` the differing pair's variant |
| mint delivery failed | `mint(&MintDeps { kem: deps.kem, key_agreement: deps.key_agreement, delivery_proof: deps.delivery_proof, random: deps.random }, MintParams, MintPayload { context: payload.mint, identity: payload.identity })` returns `Err(error)` | the call result | `mint`, once per record | `Err(ReplayErrorReturn::Mint(error))`, the error unchanged |
| transfer delivery failed | `transfer(&TransferDeps { kem: deps.kem, key_agreement: deps.key_agreement, delivery_proof: deps.delivery_proof, random: deps.random }, TransferParams, TransferPayload { context, parameter_set, identity_element, holder })` returns `Err(error)` for either settlement | the call result | `transfer`, twice per record after its mint delivery | `Err(ReplayErrorReturn::Transfer { settlement, error })`, `settlement` the `ReplaySettlement` of the failed call, the error unchanged |
| verdict failed | `deps.delivery_proof.verify` returns `Err(error)` | the call result | `deps.delivery_proof.verify`, over the transfer statement and proof, the next statement and proof, and the next statement and the transfer proof | `Err(ReplayErrorReturn::Verify { verdict, error })`, `verdict` the `ReplayVerdict` of the failed check, the error unchanged |
| computed | the next-settlement comparison holds and every record's calls succeed | the records assembled in index order | the calls stated below | `Ok(ReplaySuccessReturn { vectors })` |

### Next settlement

Before any record, comparing in `NextSettlementField` order: the `to_field(ToFieldParams, &form)` value of `payload.next.chain` with that of `payload.transfer.chain`, of `entitlement_contract` with `entitlement_contract`, the `as_bytes()` of `asset_root` with that of `asset_root`, the `as_bytes()` of `parameter_set_identifier` with that of `parameter_set_identifier`, the `to_field` value of `source_entitlement` with `source_entitlement`, of `target_entitlement` with `target_entitlement`, of `payload.next.old_interval` with `payload.transfer.new_interval`, and of `payload.next.seller` with `payload.transfer.buyer`, each `to_field` unpacked irrefutably and each pair compared by equality; the first unequal pair takes the not-the-next-settlement branch with its `NextSettlementField` variant. The suite identifier, the new interval, the purpose, the buyer, and the expiry of the next context are compared with nothing.

### Mint delivery

Per record: `mint(&MintDeps { kem: deps.kem, key_agreement: deps.key_agreement, delivery_proof: deps.delivery_proof, random: deps.random }, MintParams, MintPayload { context: payload.mint, identity: payload.identity })`, a failure taking the mint-delivery-failed branch, the admitted identity copied into each record's call, `AdmittedKemIdentity` being `Copy`.

### Transfer settlement

Per record after its mint delivery: `transfer(&TransferDeps { kem: deps.kem, key_agreement: deps.key_agreement, delivery_proof: deps.delivery_proof, random: deps.random }, TransferParams, TransferPayload { context: payload.transfer, parameter_set: &minted.delivery.parameter_set, identity_element: &minted.delivery.identity_element, holder: &minted.delivery.recipient })`, a failure taking the transfer-delivery-failed branch with `ReplaySettlement::Transfer`. The record's transfer statement and proof are `transferred.delivery.statement` and `transferred.delivery.proof`.

### Next settlement delivery

Per record after its transfer settlement: `transfer` over the same deps, `TransferParams`, and `TransferPayload { context: payload.next, parameter_set: &minted.delivery.parameter_set, identity_element: &minted.delivery.identity_element, holder: &transferred.delivery.buyer }`, a failure taking the transfer-delivery-failed branch with `ReplaySettlement::Next`. The record's next statement and proof are `next.delivery.statement` and `next.delivery.proof`; the record's draws a copy of the mint delivery's `draws`, followed by a copy of the transfer settlement's, followed by a copy of the next settlement's.

### Verdict

The shared step for a statement, a proof, and a `ReplayVerdict`: `deps.delivery_proof.verify(VerifyParams, VerifyPayload { statement: AlgebraicStatement::Transfer(&statement), proof: &proof })`, a failure taking the verdict-failed branch with the verdict; otherwise `success.is_valid`. Run over the transfer statement and the transfer proof as `ReplayVerdict::Transfer`, over the next statement and the next proof as `ReplayVerdict::Next`, and over the next statement and the transfer proof as `ReplayVerdict::Replay`, in that order, after the next settlement's proof.

### Unreached branches

No input and no failure mode of the official mocks reaches `Err(ReplayErrorReturn::Transfer { settlement, error })` for either settlement, since the mint delivery that precedes both calls every adapter a transfer delivery calls and draws from the source they use, and every mode that refuses a transfer-side call refuses the mint delivery's first; the branches have no unit block.

No input and no failure mode of the official mocks reaches `Err(ReplayErrorReturn::Verify { verdict, error })`, since the verifier's challenge and weight refusals are hash-to-scalar refusals and `MockIHashToScalarAdapterFailureMode::SamplingRefused` refuses the KEM's identity mapping or the asset setup's hash inside the mint delivery first; the branch has no unit block.

### Record

The record's outcome is a `ReplayVector` with the draws, the transfer statement, the transfer proof, its verdict as `transfer_is_valid`, the next statement, the next proof, its verdict as `next_is_valid`, and the replay's verdict as `replay_is_valid`.

### Ordering

The next-settlement comparison, then the records in index order; within a record its mint delivery, its transfer settlement, and its next settlement, then the three verdicts; the deliveries draw in the order master, `u0`, `u1`, issue, holder `x` and `y`, buyer `x` and `y`, the transfer's rerandomization, next buyer `x` and `y`, and the next settlement's rerandomization; the first failure returns; every delivery, with its witnesses and secrets, drops at the end of its record, each `Secret` cleared; no side effect beyond the deliveries' draws and the adapters' own.
