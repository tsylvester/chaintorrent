# `create_verifier` — interaction spec

Branch contract for the `factory` module of the `verifier` module of `apps/harness-crypto`: the verifier family's construction point, which selects the concrete the configuration names, admits it against the verifier form the resolved pairing declares and the delivery-statement version the params carry, constructs it over the borrowed pairing and delivery proof adapter, and hands it to a consumer generic over the family's trait for that delivery proof adapter. Each branch states condition, decision, dependency call, and the exact return outcome.

## `create_verifier<'a, P: IPairingAdapter, D: IDeliveryProofAdapter<Pairing = P>, C: IVerifierConsumer<D>>(deps: &CreateVerifierDeps<'a, P, D, C>, params: CreateVerifierParams, payload: CreateVerifierPayload) -> CreateVerifierReturn<C::Output>`

The trusted form.

Declaration: the decision is an exhaustive `match` on `&params.concrete`, so a variant with no arm fails to compile — the `Evm { .. }` arm yielding `<EvmVerifier<'a, P, D> as IVerifierAdapter>::DECLARATION` and, under `#[cfg(any(test, feature = "mocks"))]`, the `Mock(failure_mode)` arm yielding `<MockIVerifierAdapter<'a, D> as IVerifierAdapter>::DECLARATION`. The admission below runs against the declaration the arm yields.

| Branch | Condition | Decision | Dependency call | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| unsupported verifier form | the declaration's `verifier_forms` does not contain `P::DECLARATION.verifier_group_arithmetic` | slice membership, read before any construction | none | `Err(CreateVerifierErrorReturn::UnsupportedVerifierForm { form })`, `form` the pairing's; nothing constructed and the consumer not called; every concrete of the declared set and the mock concrete declare both forms, so no input takes this branch and it has no unit test |
| unsupported statement version | the form is declared and the declaration's `statement_versions` does not contain `params.statement_version` | slice membership | none | `Err(CreateVerifierErrorReturn::UnsupportedStatementVersion { version })`, `version` the params'; nothing constructed and the consumer not called |
| constructor refused | the form and version are declared and `try_new` returns `Err(error)` | the selected concrete's constructor runs once | `EvmVerifier::try_new(EvmVerifierConstructorParams { pairing: deps.pairing, delivery_proof: deps.delivery_proof, settings: *settings })` | `Err(CreateVerifierErrorReturn::Evm(error))`, the refusal unchanged and the consumer not called |
| admitted | `try_new` returns `Ok(adapter)` | the adapter moves into the consumer's payload | `deps.consumer.consume_verifier(ConsumeVerifierParams, ConsumeVerifierPayload { adapter })`, exactly once | `Ok(CreateVerifierSuccessReturn { output })` holding the consumer's output |

`Mock(failure_mode)` arm, under `#[cfg(any(test, feature = "mocks"))]`: the same admission over `<MockIVerifierAdapter<'a, D> as IVerifierAdapter>::DECLARATION` — `verifier_forms` against the pairing's form, then `statement_versions` against `params.statement_version`, each read before any construction and each refusal the family's flavor the table's rows return; then `MockIVerifierAdapter::try_new(MockIVerifierAdapterConstructorParams { failure_mode, delivery_proof: deps.delivery_proof })`, exactly once, `failure_mode` the mode the `Mock` member of `params.concrete` carries, its success destructured by `let Ok(adapter) = …;` because its error arm is `Infallible`; then `deps.consumer.consume_verifier(ConsumeVerifierParams, ConsumeVerifierPayload { adapter })`, exactly once; the outcome is `Ok(CreateVerifierSuccessReturn { output })` holding the consumer's output.

## Ordering and invariants

- The declaration's selection, then the verifier form check, then the statement version check, then construction, then the consumer — the first refusal returns.
- `params.concrete` selects the concrete and carries its settings or failure mode; `params.statement_version` admits; `payload` carries nothing and is not read.
- The deps' borrows are copied into the constructor params; the consumer is generic over `IVerifierAdapter` whose `DeliveryProof` is the caller's delivery proof adapter type, and never names the concrete.
- Adding a verifier concrete is its module, its variant in `VerifierConcrete`, its entry in `VerifierConcreteKind`, `VERIFIER_CONCRETES`, and the declared-selection builder, its variant in `CreateVerifierErrorReturn`, and its branch here — no consumer changes; the factory names no endpoint, address, curve, verifier form value, or version value.

## Integration: own entries

Each entry is proven by the private integration block of the same name in `integration_test.rs`, except `a_statement_version_no_concrete_declares_is_refused_and_the_declared_version_admitted` and `an_adapter_handed_to_the_consumer_declares_the_pairings_verifier_form_and_the_params_statement_version`, proven by the public integration block of the same name in `tests/verifier_integration_test.rs`; `every_concrete_refuses_a_proof_of_the_other_relation_before_any_chain_read` is proven in both.

- `every_concrete_refuses_a_proof_of_the_other_relation_before_any_chain_read`: condition `verify_on_chain` on the adapter `create_verifier` hands the consumer for a member of `VERIFIER_CONCRETES`, selected by `build_verifier_concrete` at its default settings, and for the mock selection under `NoFailure`, over a real mint statement with the real proof `prove_transfer` returned, and over a real transfer statement with the real proof `prove_mint` returned; outcome `Err(VerifyOnChainErrorReturn::RelationMismatch { statement, proof })` with the relations the case names, asserted by a pattern naming every field because `VerifyOnChainErrorReturn` omits `PartialEq`; variation every member of `VERIFIER_CONCRETES` and the mock selection, crossed with every member of `PAIRING_CONCRETES` and both orders; edge that must survive the composition: the refusal is the family's flavor, decided from the payload by every concrete the factory constructs before any chain read, so the default endpoint, a loopback address nothing listens at, is never reached.
- `a_refused_read_returns_the_mock_concretes_own_error`: condition `verify_on_chain` on the adapter `create_verifier` hands the consumer for `VerifierConcrete::Mock` carrying `MockIVerifierAdapterFailureMode::RpcRefused`, over a real mint statement and the real proof `prove_mint` returned; outcome `Err(VerifyOnChainErrorReturn::Mock(MockIVerifierAdapterVerifyOnChainErrorReturn::RpcRefused))`, asserted by a pattern naming every field; variation the failure mode against `NoFailure`; edge the mode is an error arm no input reaches, selected only through configuration.
- `an_adapter_handed_to_the_consumer_declares_the_pairings_verifier_form_and_the_params_statement_version`: condition `create_verifier` over a member of `VERIFIER_CONCRETES`, selected by `build_verifier_concrete` at its default settings, at `DELIVERY_STATEMENT_VERSION_ONE`, with a consumer that reads `V::DECLARATION` of the adapter it receives; outcome `Ok(CreateVerifierSuccessReturn { output: () })`, the consumer having found that the declaration's `verifier_forms` contains `P::DECLARATION.verifier_group_arithmetic`, its `statement_versions` contains `DELIVERY_STATEMENT_VERSION_ONE`, and its `interface_version` equals `VERIFIER_INTERFACE_VERSION`; variation every member of `VERIFIER_CONCRETES` crossed with every member of `PAIRING_CONCRETES`, whose BN254 concretes declare `FirstGroupOnly` and whose BLS12-381 concretes declare `BothGroups`, so a factory that hands an adapter not serving the pairing's form, or none, fails; edge the adapter is constructed before the consumer runs and the consumer's output reaches the return unaltered.
- `a_statement_version_no_concrete_declares_is_refused_and_the_declared_version_admitted`: condition `create_verifier` over a member of `VERIFIER_CONCRETES`, selected by `build_verifier_concrete` at its default settings, with `statement_version` `2` and with `DELIVERY_STATEMENT_VERSION_ONE`, and a consumer whose output is `()`; outcome `Err(CreateVerifierErrorReturn::UnsupportedStatementVersion { version: 2 })` for `2` and `Ok(CreateVerifierSuccessReturn { output: () })` for version one, each asserted by a pattern; variation every member of `VERIFIER_CONCRETES` crossed with every member of `PAIRING_CONCRETES`, and the two versions; edge the version is admitted against the real concrete's declaration, read through the factory and never named by the caller.

## Integration: private surface

## Integration: public surface

An outside caller invokes `create_verifier` with `CreateVerifierParams` from `harness_crypto`, a consumer implementing `IVerifierConsumer`, and the pairing and delivery proof adapter the other families' factories hand it, selecting the concrete from `VERIFIER_CONCRETES`, and calls `verify_on_chain` on the adapter its consumer receives; it observes the `CreateVerifierReturn` and, inside its consumer, the received adapter's declaration and the `VerifyOnChainReturn` through `IVerifierAdapter`; the entries proven are `a_statement_version_no_concrete_declares_is_refused_and_the_declared_version_admitted`, `an_adapter_handed_to_the_consumer_declares_the_pairings_verifier_form_and_the_params_statement_version`, and `every_concrete_refuses_a_proof_of_the_other_relation_before_any_chain_read`, over `VERIFIER_CONCRETES` alone.
