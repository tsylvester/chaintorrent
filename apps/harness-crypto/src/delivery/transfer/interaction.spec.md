# `transfer` — interaction spec

Branch contract for the `transfer` module of the `delivery` module of `apps/harness-crypto`: `transfer` produces the transfer delivery, generic over the credential KEM, the key agreement, and the delivery proof with its chain forms its caller hands it — over a typed transfer context, the parameter set, the identity element, and the holder it transfers from: the holder's envelope opened under the holder's key pair, the buyer's key pair generated and its public keys admitted with their proof of possession, the opened credential rerandomized, the result wrapped to the buyer's keys, and the transfer statement proved; it returns the statement and proof, the rerandomized credential, the witnesses the prover took, the buyer as a holder valid for the next transfer, and the uniforms it drew. Each branch states condition, decision, dependency call, and the exact return outcome. `S` below is `P::Scalar`.

## `transfer`

`transfer<'a, P: IPairingAdapter, K: ICredentialKemAdapter<Pairing = P>, A: IKeyAgreementAdapter<Pairing = P>, D: IDeliveryProofAdapter<Pairing = P>>(deps: &TransferDeps<'a, K, A, D>, params: TransferParams, payload: TransferPayload<'_, K, A, D::Forms>) -> TransferReturn<P, K, A, D>`, the trusted form: every input is a typed value from the caller — a borrowed KEM, key agreement, delivery proof, and randomness source in `deps`, and the transfer context, the parameter set, the identity element, and the holder in `payload`.

| Branch | Condition | Decision | Dependency call | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| draw failed | `deps.random.fill_bytes(FillBytesParams, FillBytesPayload { length })` returns `Err(error)`, `length` `<S as ISampleUniformScalar>::UNIFORM_BYTES_LENGTH` | the call result | `deps.random.fill_bytes`, once per uniform | `Err(TransferErrorReturn::FillBytes(error))`, the error unchanged |
| key generation failed | `deps.key_agreement.generate_keys` returns `Err(error)` | the call result | `deps.key_agreement.generate_keys` | `Err(TransferErrorReturn::GenerateKeys(error))`, the error unchanged |
| key admission failed | `deps.key_agreement.public_keys_from_components` returns `Err(error)` | the call result | `deps.key_agreement.public_keys_from_components` | `Err(TransferErrorReturn::PublicKeysFromComponents(error))`, the error unchanged |
| rerandomization failed | `deps.kem.rerandomize` returns `Err(error)` | the call result | `deps.kem.rerandomize` | `Err(TransferErrorReturn::Rerandomize(error))`, the error unchanged |
| wrap failed | `deps.key_agreement.wrap_to` returns `Err(error)` | the call result | `deps.key_agreement.wrap_to` | `Err(TransferErrorReturn::WrapTo(error))`, the error unchanged |
| proof failed | `deps.delivery_proof.prove_transfer` returns `Err(error)` | the call result | `deps.delivery_proof.prove_transfer` | `Err(TransferErrorReturn::ProveTransfer(error))`, the error unchanged |
| computed | every call succeeds | the delivery assembled | the draws and adapter calls stated below | `Ok(TransferSuccessReturn { delivery })` |

### Draw

Every draw is `deps.random.fill_bytes(FillBytesParams, FillBytesPayload { length: <S as ISampleUniformScalar>::UNIFORM_BYTES_LENGTH })`; a failure takes the draw-failed branch. A copy of `success.bytes.expose()` is appended to the delivery's `draws` and `success.bytes` is handed on as the uniform.

### Copy of a secret

A copy of a `Secret` is `Secret::try_new(SecretConstructorParams { value: secret.expose().clone() })`, `Result<_, Infallible>` unpacked irrefutably, the scalar being `Clone`; the copy is kept and the original handed on.

### Opening

`deps.key_agreement.unwrap(UnwrapParams, UnwrapPayload { key_pair: &payload.holder.key_pair, envelope: &payload.holder.envelope })`, `Result<_, Infallible>` unpacked irrefutably; `deps.kem.credential_from_components` over the opened components, `Result<_, Infallible>` unpacked irrefutably, the seller's credential from its fresh opening.

### Buyer

A first and a second draw as `x_uniform` and `y_uniform`; `deps.key_agreement.generate_keys(GenerateKeysParams, GenerateKeysPayload { x_uniform, y_uniform })`, a failure taking the key-generation-failed branch; `deps.key_agreement.key_pair_public_keys` over the key pair, `Result<_, Infallible>` unpacked irrefutably; `deps.key_agreement.public_keys_from_components(PublicKeysFromComponentsParams, PublicKeysFromComponentsPayload { components, possession: &generated.possession })`, `components` the public keys just read, a failure taking the key-admission-failed branch. No input and no failure mode of the official mocks reaches this arm, since the keys admitted are the ones `generate_keys` has just formed, with their own proof of possession.

### Rerandomization

A third draw; `deps.kem.rerandomize(RerandomizeParams, RerandomizePayload { parameter_set: payload.parameter_set, identity_element: payload.identity_element, credential: &opened, uniform })`, a failure taking the rerandomization-failed branch. No input and no failure mode of the official mocks reaches this arm, since the draw is of the length the sampling bound declares.

### Wrap

`deps.kem.credential_components` over the rerandomized credential, `Result<_, Infallible>` unpacked irrefutably; `deps.key_agreement.wrap_to(WrapToParams, WrapToPayload { public_keys: &admitted.public_keys, credential: components })`, a failure taking the wrap-failed branch.

### Statement

`deps.kem.identity_element_components` over `payload.identity_element`, `deps.key_agreement.public_keys_components` over `payload.holder.public_keys`, `deps.key_agreement.key_pair_public_keys` over the buyer's key pair, and `deps.key_agreement.envelope_components` over `payload.holder.envelope` and over the new envelope, each `Result<_, Infallible>` unpacked irrefutably; `AlgebraicTransferStatement { context: payload.context.clone(), identity_element: element, seller_keys: the holder's public-key components, buyer_keys: the buyer's public-key components, old_envelope: the holder envelope's components, new_envelope: the new envelope's components }`.

### Witnesses

`deps.key_agreement.key_pair_components` over `payload.holder.key_pair`, `Result<_, Infallible>` unpacked irrefutably, called twice, once for the prover and once for the witnesses; the rerandomization offset and each of the wrap's coins copied as above, the copy kept for the witnesses and the original handed to the prover; the offset is the total offset, the holder carrying none of its own.

### Proof

`deps.delivery_proof.prove_transfer(ProveTransferParams, ProveTransferPayload { seller_secrets, offset: rerandomized.offset, coins: wrap.coins, statement: &statement })`, a failure taking the proof-failed branch.

### Computed

`Ok(TransferSuccessReturn { delivery })`, a `TransferDelivery` with `statement`, `proof`, `rerandomized.credential` as `credential`, the `witnesses`, `DeliveryHolder { key_pair, public_keys, envelope }` as `buyer`, and the `draws`.

### Ordering

The opening; then the draws in the order buyer `x`, buyer `y`, rerandomization, each immediately before its consuming call, so key generation and admission precede the rerandomization; then the wrap, the statement, the witnesses, and the proof; the first failure returns; the opened credential is dropped with the call's scope; every uniform's and secret's `Secret` is cleared when it drops; no side effect beyond the draws and the adapters' own.

## Integration: own entries

Each entry is proven by the public integration block of the same name in `tests/transfer_integration_test.rs`, the private integration test element proving no own entry.

- `every_transfer_delivery_is_a_statement_and_proof_the_delivery_proof_verifies`: condition `mint` over a case, then `transfer` over that delivery's parameter set, identity element, and recipient, every adapter real and the operating-system source real; outcome `verify` over `AlgebraicStatement::Transfer(&delivery.statement)` and `&delivery.proof` returns `is_valid` true; variation every case, each member of `PAIRING_CONCRETES` crossed with the selections the other families declare and each scope of `DECLARED_KEM_SELECTIONS`, each of `TransferPurpose::Transfer` and `TransferPurpose::Grant`, so both curves, both verifier forms, both scopes, and both purposes run; edge the statement is built from the holder's and the buyer's own objects, so a statement over another envelope or other keys reports otherwise.
- `the_witnesses_prove_the_statement_again`: condition `transfer` over a case, then `prove_transfer` called on the case's delivery proof over the delivery's witnesses and statement; outcome the second proof verifies against the statement; variation every case; edge the witnesses are copies independent of the values the delivery's own proof consumed.
- `the_buyer_opens_its_envelope_to_a_credential_valid_for_the_identity`: condition `transfer` over a case, then `unwrap` of `delivery.buyer.envelope` under `delivery.buyer.key_pair`, `credential_from_components` over the result, and `is_valid` under the payload's parameter set and identity element; outcome `is_valid` true, and the opened credential's components equal those of `delivery.credential`; variation every case; edge the envelope is the one the statement names as its new envelope.
- `a_transfer_from_a_transfers_buyer_is_a_transfer_the_delivery_proof_verifies`: condition `transfer` over a case, then `transfer` again over the same parameter set and identity element with `&delivery.buyer` as the holder; outcome `verify` over the second delivery's statement and proof returns `is_valid` true, its statement's `seller_keys` equal the first statement's `buyer_keys`, and its `old_envelope` equals the first statement's `new_envelope`; variation every case; edge the returned buyer is a complete holder, so a holder missing its admitted keys or its envelope cannot transfer.
- `every_draw_replays_to_the_objects_it_produced`: condition `transfer` over a case, the draws split at `UNIFORM_BYTES_LENGTH` into the uniforms of its calls in draw order, `generate_keys` run on the case's key agreement over the first two, and `rerandomize` run on the case's KEM over the third with the credential `unwrap` and `credential_from_components` open from the holder; outcome the replayed key pair's `pk1` and `pk2` and the replayed credential's `a` and `b`, each encoded through the case's pairing, equal those of the delivery's buyer keys and credential, and the draws are three uniforms long; variation every case; edge draws in another order replay to other objects.
