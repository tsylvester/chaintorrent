# `encoding_consumer` — interaction spec

Branch contract for the `encoding_consumer` module of the `run` module of `apps/harness-crypto`: `EncodingConsumer` is the run's consumer of the encoding family's factory — an `IEncodingConsumer` generic over the pairing, constructed from the borrowed pairing, hash-to-scalar adapter, and randomness source, the borrowed `AdmittedStatements` and `AdmittedIdentities`, the admitted chain forms, KEM, and key agreement selections, the delivery proof selection, the generate selection with its per-kind configuration, the verifier concrete of the curve the consumer composes, the statement version, and the iteration count, which on receiving the encoder the factory constructed builds a `ChainFormsConsumer` over a borrow of it, calls `create_chain_forms` once with the held chain forms selection and a borrow of that same encoder, and returns the files written and the measurement, or the first refusal whole.
It names no concrete, reads no configuration, and admits no selection or identity.
Each branch states condition, decision, dependency call, and the exact return outcome.

## `EncodingConsumer::try_new`

`EncodingConsumer::try_new(params: EncodingConsumerConstructorParams<'a, P>) -> EncodingConsumerTryNewReturn<'a, P>`: the one producer of the consumer; the adapters, the admitted statements, the admitted identities, and the configured values are required at creation, and no partially constructed consumer exists.

### Constructed

Outcome `Ok(EncodingConsumer)` holding the params' adapters, statements, identities, and configured values; the error arm is `Infallible` and has no branch.

## `consume_encoding`

`consume_encoding<E: IEncoderAdapter + IDecoderAdapter>(&self, _params: ConsumeEncodingParams, payload: ConsumeEncodingPayload<E>) -> EncodingConsumerReturn`, the trait method of `IEncodingConsumer for EncodingConsumer<'a, P>`, `type Output = EncodingConsumerReturn`; the params carry nothing and are not read, and the payload's adapter is the encoder the forms concrete is constructed over, which the consumer borrows.

### Forms refused

Dependency call `create_chain_forms(&CreateChainFormsDeps { consumer, encoder: &payload.adapter }, CreateChainFormsParams { concrete: self.chain_forms.concrete, identifier: self.chain_forms.identifier }, CreateChainFormsPayload)`, once, `consumer` the `ChainFormsConsumer` built by `ChainFormsConsumer::try_new(ChainFormsConsumerConstructorParams { pairing: self.pairing, hash_to_scalar: self.hash_to_scalar, encoder: &payload.adapter, random: self.random, statements: self.statements, identities: self.identities, kem: self.kem, key_agreement: self.key_agreement, delivery_proof: self.delivery_proof, generate: self.generate.clone(), kinds: self.kinds.clone(), verifier: self.verifier.clone(), statement_version: self.statement_version, iterations: self.iterations })` through the irrefutable pattern `let Ok(consumer) = …;`; condition the call returns `Err(error)`; outcome `Err(EncodingConsumerErrorReturn::CreateChainForms(error))`, `error` unchanged.

### Forms consumer refused

Condition the call returns `Ok(CreateChainFormsSuccessReturn { output })` and `output` is `Err(error)`; outcome `Err(EncodingConsumerErrorReturn::ChainForms(error))`, `error` unchanged.

### Consumed

Condition `output` is `Ok(success)`; outcome `Ok(success)`, the `DeliveryProofConsumerSuccessReturn` as the delivery proof consumer returned it through the key agreement, KEM, and chain forms consumers; the method compares, converts, and summarizes nothing.

### Ordering

The `ChainFormsConsumer` is built before `create_chain_forms` is called, and `create_chain_forms` is called once; the encoder the consumer is built over and the encoder the deps lend are the one borrow of `payload.adapter`; the first refusal returns and nothing after it runs; the held values are borrowed or cloned for the call and not altered.
