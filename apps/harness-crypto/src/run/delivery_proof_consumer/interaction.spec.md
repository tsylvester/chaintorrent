## Ordering and invariants

- The generate consumer's construction, then the one call to `create_generate`; `params` carries nothing and is not read; there is no side effect of the consumer's own, every write being the generate concrete's.
- The consumer calls `create_generate` and no other factory, reads no clock, touches no filesystem itself, and holds no literal; every value it hands `create_generate` is its own configured value, a borrow the earlier factories handed, or the handed adapter.
- The consumer serves every forms type, so a caller of `create_delivery_proof` names `F` in its call.
