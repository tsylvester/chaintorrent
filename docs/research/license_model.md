# ChainTorrent License Model

## Status and scope

This document records the current licensing model derived during protocol research. It is a design record, not legal advice or final license text. It concerns ChainTorrent's own source code, patent claims, protocol participation, and implementation conformance. It does not prescribe the licenses or prices that publishers use for their own repositories or content.

## Conclusion

ChainTorrent should use a bespoke source-available commercial license paired with a commercial patent and protocol license. It should not use an OSI open-source, permissive, or copyleft license for the ChainTorrent implementation.

The reason is structural: ChainTorrent's access-entitlement enforcement is necessary for commercial publishers and distributors to participate. A license that permits a modified implementation to remove that enforcement and redistribute the result cannot preserve the protocol's economic model. Copyright conditions govern copied ChainTorrent source, but not a clean-room interoperable implementation. Patent claims are therefore needed to make conformance obligations reach commercially deployed, protocol-compatible reimplementations.

## Public source availability

ChainTorrent may remain publicly hosted on GitHub. Public source visibility does not grant unrestricted commercial rights. The source-available license should grant no-fee rights to inspect, evaluate, study, and develop the source within its stated limits.

Commercial deployment or operation of a ChainTorrent-compatible implementation requires the separate commercial patent and protocol license. A later move to swarm-hosted primary distribution does not change this distinction.

## Commercial patent and protocol license

The commercial patent and protocol license grants the right to practice the claimed ChainTorrent methods and to participate in the production network only while the implementation conforms to the protocol requirements.

Conformance is required for all production-network participation, regardless of whether the implementation earns revenue. Revenue determines payment, not permission.

- A modified client may be studied and tested outside the production network.
- A conforming production-network client that generates no in-scope consideration may be royalty-free.
- A commercially active production-network client must use the canonical transaction waterfall and preserve its required floors.
- A client that removes or bypasses entitlement enforcement has no production-network patent/protocol license. Calling that activity non-commercial research does not create an exemption.

The license must condition commercial practice on preserving access-control behavior, including protections against bypassing access checks, retaining decryption material beyond authorization, or enabling entitlement-free decryption and redistribution.

## Protocol-level preservation requirements

Every licensed commercial implementation must preserve the following protocol-level minimums:

- Entitlement portability: a valid entitlement can be exercised through any conforming implementation.
- Holder control: a platform may not confiscate, automatically repurchase, custody-lock, or extinguish an entitlement because an over-the-top subscription ends.
- Open swarm participation: eligible seeders and verifiers/validators may participate under the protocol rules and receive their required allocation.
- Protocol maintenance: each in-scope entitlement transaction allocates the protocol's required minimum share.
- Non-discrimination by client or platform: a valid entitlement is usable through conforming implementations, subject only to necessary security and anti-fraud measures.

These obligations prevent a platform from using ChainTorrent as private distribution infrastructure while capturing public swarm contributions and confining entitlements to a walled garden.

## Economics and royalty model

ChainTorrent is neutral infrastructure, but its maintenance and the protocol's public work must be funded. Patent royalties are modeled as the protocol-maintenance portion of the economic split generated by entitlement activity, rather than as a separate per-copy charge.

The durable payment trigger is consideration received in connection with a ChainTorrent entitlement transaction, whether direct or indirect. The transaction waterfall includes floors for protocol maintenance and public swarm work, with the remaining allocation available for publisher-selected economics. The protocol does not require identical pricing for every user and does not prohibit fixed prices, auctions, reverse auctions, Dutch auctions, subscriber discounts, subscriber-only primary sales, or other price-discovery mechanisms.

An over-the-top subscription may charge for additional services and may influence the price at which a subscriber receives a newly minted entitlement. It may not reach down into the protocol layer to limit the use, transfer, or resale of an entitlement that the subscriber already holds.

## Entitlements, distributions, and versions

An access entitlement is bound to ownership of the immutable entitlement object that embodies it. A holder who can prove current possession retains the entitlement. The current protocol model is ownership-based; it does not presently provide a rental entitlement type.

Existing entitlements are not retroactively changed by later license terms or protocol changes. A future rental or lending model must be introduced as a new entitlement class or version, not by reducing the rights of an existing holder.

The protocol distinguishes a distribution from a version:

- A distribution is the same object/version reissued under a different prospective key policy or license offer.
- A version is a distinct work for which the publisher may set separate entitlement and upgrade terms.

When a publisher reissues the same version as a new distribution, it is contractually obligated to reissue an existing key to an existing keyholder who elects to transition. The holder may instead retain the current distribution and current key. A publisher may decide which version increments existing keyholders receive, including providing bug-fix increments without charge while requiring a new key for a later feature version.

Pricing applies to newly issued entitlements or versions; it cannot deny continued use of a valid existing entitlement.

## License issuance and distributor identity

Production-network participation should be conditional by compliance, with registration and notice implemented as an active protocol element.

The production handshake should identify the distributor of the client distribution, not the end user or the operator of an individual node. A distributor credential binds:

- the distributor's legal identity and service-for-notice route;
- a distributor signing key;
- an immutable release manifest containing client artifact hashes and relevant platform/backend/head variants;
- the protocol-spec revision and patent/protocol terms under which the distribution claims conformance; and
- issuance, expiry, and revocation information.

Finder/bootstrap participants can verify the credential, signature, validity, and revocation status before admitting a production client. Presenting the credential and signed conformance declaration supplies an affirmative acceptance and an evidentiary record for the patent/protocol license.

This is analogous to a certificate system, but it certifies the legally accountable distributor of a specific client distribution. It is distinct from the later concerns of node-operator identity and end-user/entitlement-holder identity.

No certificate can prove an untrusted binary has not been modified without a trusted execution environment. Its role is legal and operational: legitimate distributors are identifiable, accept the terms, can be audited or revoked, and leave evidence if their released implementation is non-conforming. An unlicensed modification cannot obtain its own valid distributor credential.

### MVP boundary

For the MVP, ChainTorrent is the only anticipated client distributor and no public production release is available. The immediate requirement is therefore the resolved protocol position: every production client distribution identifies its accountable distributor through a registration-backed credential. Designing issuance workflows for licensed alternative distributors, certificate delegation, and multi-distributor governance is deferred until an alternative distributor is a real implementation need.

## Deferred matters

The following are intentionally not settled here:

- Exact patent claims, their jurisdictional strategy, and prosecution.
- Final license wording, remedies, governing law, notice procedure, and enforceability analysis.
- Exact fee percentages and allocation formulas among publishers, seeders, verifiers/validators, and protocol maintenance.
- End-user identity, node-operator identity, and federated-node identity.
- Alternative-distributor credential issuance, delegation, and governance workflows.
- Governance transition from current maintainers to a foundation.
- Rental, lending, and other future entitlement types.
